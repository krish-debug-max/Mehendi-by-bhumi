Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking Bhoomi for your wedding or party.

How does pricing work for Sangeet and guest parties?
For Sangeet nights and party events, pricing is based on a convenient **hourly rate** rather than per hand. This allows maximum guests to get their mehendi done efficiently within your reserved timeframe!
Is your henna organic and safe for baby shower belly mehendi?
Yes! Bhoomi hand-prepares 100% natural organic henna paste using pure essential oils (eucalyptus & clove). It is completely safe, gentle, chemical-free, and suitable for expectant mothers and sensitive skin.
Do you offer non-traditional mehendi designs for Girls' Night Out or Birthdays?
Absolutely! We love non-traditional henna placement like shoulder motifs, back designs, wrists, and modern boho patterns—perfect for bonding with your girlfriends.
How far in advance should I book my bridal mehendi date?
Bridal slots during peak wedding season fill up quickly. We recommend booking 2 to 4 months in advance to lock in your preferred date and time slot.
Can Bhoomi travel to destination wedding venues?
Yes, Bhoomi and her team travel for outstation wedding ceremonies, corporate events, and celebrations across India.
